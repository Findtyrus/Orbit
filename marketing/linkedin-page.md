## Overview (the About section)

```
Your career has gravity. The people you stay close to pull opportunities toward you. Orbit keeps them close.

Orbit is a networking app for students recruiting into finance and accounting: audit, tax, advisory, valuation, investment banking and corporate finance.

Every coffee chat ends with a promise. "Send me your resume." "Reach back out in January." Most of them get forgotten. Orbit makes sure they don't.

What Orbit does:
• Today: a short list of who to reach out to each morning, with why now and what to do next
• Outreach queue: follow-ups, check-ins and intros, already drafted. You read, edit and send every one yourself
• Relationship memory: what each person told you, what you promised, and what you have in common
• Companies: your recruiting pipeline, with who you know at every firm and when applications are due
• Meeting prep: a one page brief before every coffee chat

Import your free LinkedIn data export and Orbit works from day one. It runs on your phone, in light or dark mode.

Your data is private to you, and you can export or delete it anytime. Orbit never sends a message on your behalf.

Built by an accounting student at Mississippi State. Free for 14 days, then $6 a month.
```

## Set up checklist

1. Create the page: Me, then Create a Company Page, then Company.
2. Fill in every field above. LinkedIn ranks complete pages higher in search.
3. Add Orbit to your own Experience as Founder, so you show up as an employee and the logo appears on your profile.
4. Set the custom button to Sign up.
5. Invite your connections to follow. LinkedIn gives free invite credits every month; start with classmates and people in finance and accounting.
6. Post the first three posts below, then pin the promo video.
