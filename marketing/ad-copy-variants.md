# Orbit ad copy variants

Everything points to **buildyourorbit.com**. Rules for every line: no dashes, only real numbers (14 days, $6 a
month, $48 a year), no made up testimonials or user counts, and never imply Orbit sends messages for you.

**How to test:** pick 3 angles, run 2 or 3 hooks each with the matching video ad, and spend about $15 per angle.
Kill anything that isn't under $5 per trial after $15. Then test hooks and headlines inside the winning angle.

**Tag every link** so you know what worked:
`https://buildyourorbit.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=promise&utm_content=hook2`

Contents: 10 angles (Meta, TikTok, hooks) · YouTube and TV scripts · Google search ads · LinkedIn · Reddit and
GroupMe · CTAs and offer lines
