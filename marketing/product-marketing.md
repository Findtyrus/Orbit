# Orbit product marketing brief

The single reference for every piece of copy, ad and post. Give this file to any AI tool before asking it to
write marketing for Orbit. Rule: no em or en dashes anywhere, only real numbers and real quotes.

## The product in one line
Orbit is a networking CRM for students recruiting into finance and accounting. It remembers every coffee chat and
tells you who to follow up with, and what to say.

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

**Primary:** a college sophomore, junior or senior (or master's in accounting student) recruiting for internships or
full-time roles in audit, tax, transaction advisory, valuation, investment banking, corporate finance or private
equity. Most likely at a non-target school, where networking is the main way in.

What their life looks like:
- 50 to 500 LinkedIn connections, a handful of real conversations, and a messy spreadsheet or Notes app
- Coffee chats every week during recruiting season, on top of 15 to 18 credit hours and often a job
- Constantly told "networking is everything" with no system for doing it

**Their pains, in their words** (verify with real students, then update):
- "I forget what people told me on coffee chats."
- "I never know when it's okay to follow up."
- "I connected with 300 people and actually talk to 5."
- "I don't know who I know at the firm I'm applying to."
- "I said I'd send my resume and then forgot."

**Who it's not for (yet):** experienced professionals, sales teams, recruiters. Saying no keeps the message sharp.

**Secondary:** any business student recruiting (consulting, sales, supply chain). Same product, broader message later.

## Outcomes (lead with these, not features)
1. **Turn coffee chats into referrals.** The follow-up is what gets the referral; Orbit makes sure it happens.
2. **Never look unprepared.** A brief before every chat and a memory of every past conversation.
3. **Know exactly who to talk to today.** A short ranked list instead of a 700 person mess.
4. **Know who you know at every company you're applying to.**

## Features that back the outcomes
| Outcome | Feature |
|---|---|
| Referrals | Daily outreach queue, open loops ("send resume to Marcus"), follow-up reminders |
| Prepared | Relationship memory per person, meeting prep briefs, "In common" (shared employer, school, path) |
| Who to talk to | Today feed ranked by why now, relationship strength score |
| Who you know where | Companies pipeline: target firms, who you know there, next move |

## The offer
- **Free for 14 days**, no credit card, with a limited taste of the AI
- **Pro:** $6 a month or $48 a year (about $4 a month), less than one coffee a month
- **Works on your phone:** add it to your home screen from Safari
- **You stay in control:** Orbit never sends anything for you; every message is a draft you send

## Objections and answers (use in the FAQ and in replies)
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is my data safe?" | Your data is private to your account. You can export or delete everything anytime. The AI provider doesn't train on it. |
| "Will it message people for me?" | Never. Orbit drafts; you read, edit and send every message yourself. |
| "Do I need LinkedIn Premium?" | No. You import your free LinkedIn data export. |
| "LinkedIn export takes a day." | Add people by hand while you wait. Orbit works from day one. |
| "Is it only for finance and accounting?" | Built for them first, but any student who networks can use it. |
| "Why not a spreadsheet?" | A spreadsheet doesn't remember what people said, tell you when to follow up, or draft the message. |
| "Is it worth paying for?" | $48 a year is less than one interview outfit. One referral pays for it many times over. |
| "Is it an App Store app?" | It's a web app you add to your home screen. Same feel, no download. |

## Positioning against alternatives
| Alternative | Why Orbit instead |
|---|---|
| Spreadsheet or Notes | Remembers context, reminds you, drafts the follow-up |
| LinkedIn itself | LinkedIn shows connections; Orbit tracks relationships and what to do next |
| Dex, Clay and other personal CRMs | Built for professionals; Orbit is built for student recruiting (companies pipeline, coffee chats, deadlines) and priced for students |
| Handshake | Handshake is for finding jobs; Orbit is for the people who get you hired |

## Ad angles to test
Each angle is one problem and one outcome. Make 2 creatives per angle (a static image from the demo account and a
short video), then keep the angle that gets the cheapest trial.

1. **The forgotten promise.** "You said you'd send your resume. Did you?" Shows open loops.
2. **The follow-up gap.** "The coffee chat doesn't get you the referral. The follow-up does." Shows outreach queue.
3. **Who do you know?** "Applying to Deloitte? You probably already know someone there." Shows Companies.
4. **The 300 connections problem.** "300 connections. 5 real relationships." Shows strength scores.
5. **Never unprepared.** "Walk into every coffee chat knowing exactly what to ask." Shows meeting prep.
6. **Built by a student.** "I'm an accounting student. I built the networking app I needed." Founder story.

## Channels, in order
1. Personal messages to classmates and club members (free, highest conversion)
2. Campus: Beta Alpha Psi, finance and investment clubs, professors, career center, Entrepreneurship Center
3. LinkedIn founder posts and short videos (TikTok, Reels) from the demo account
4. Small paid test after organic proof: about $50 on Instagram and TikTok to students 18 to 24 interested in
   accounting and finance, near your campus first. Success = cost per trial under $5 and cost per paying customer
   under $25. Stop any ad that isn't there after $15.

## Numbers to watch
Cost per sign-up, sign-up to LinkedIn import, trial to paid, and where each user heard about Orbit.
